# Product Warranty

This page explains how faults are handled for the products you buy from PawPantry, in particular our **PawPantry Pro** devices: the AquaFlow water fountain, the SmartBowl automatic feeder and the ComfortHeat heated pet bed.

## Legal guarantee (all products)

Every product sold to consumers in the EU is covered by the **2-year legal guarantee of conformity**, counted from the delivery date. If a product is defective or does not match its description within that period, we repair it, replace it or, if neither is possible, refund it. This guarantee is free and does not require registration.

## PawPantry Pro extended warranty

PawPantry Pro devices get **one extra year** of warranty — **3 years in total from delivery** — when you register the device within **30 days of delivery** at pawpantry.example/register. You need your order number and the serial number printed under the base of the device. Devices that are not registered are covered by the 2-year legal guarantee only.

The extended warranty covers the pump, motor, electronics, power adapter and app connectivity of the device.

## What is not covered

- Damage caused by chewing, scratching or biting.
- Consumables: filters, foam pre-filters and replacement bowls.
- **Batteries**: covered for **6 months** only.
- Damage caused by not following the care guide, for example running the fountain pump dry or putting parts that are not dishwasher-safe in a dishwasher.
- Normal wear and cosmetic scratches that do not affect how the product works.
- Products bought from other sellers, including marketplace sellers, even if they are PawPantry-branded.

## How to make a warranty claim

1. Go to **My account → Orders**, select the order and click **Report a problem**. If you bought the product as a guest, contact customer care with your order number.
2. Describe the problem and attach a **short video** (30 seconds is enough) showing it. For feeders and fountains, please show the device switched on.
3. Our technical team replies within **2 business days**. Many problems can be solved remotely — for example a feeder reset or a pump cleaning.
4. If the claim is approved, we send a free return label. A repaired or replacement device is shipped within **10 business days** of approval. If a repair or replacement is not possible, we refund the price you paid.

## Warranty after a repair or replacement

A repair or replacement does not restart the warranty. The repaired or replacement device is covered for the remainder of the original warranty period or for **6 months**, whichever is longer.

## Accidental damage

Accidental damage (a dropped fountain, a cable chewed by a puppy) is not covered by the warranty. We can still help: replacement parts such as power adapters, pumps and bowls are sold separately, and our team can tell you which part you need.

## Products from other brands

Products from other brands that we sell are covered by the 2-year legal guarantee through us. Some manufacturers offer an additional warranty directly; the details are on the product page and in the box. For those extra warranties, please contact the manufacturer.
