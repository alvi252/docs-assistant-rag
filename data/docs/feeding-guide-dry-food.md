# Feeding Guide — PawPantry Kitchen Dry Food

This guide gives daily amounts for our **PawPantry Kitchen** dry food for adult dogs and cats. The amounts are starting points: every pet is different, so adjust gradually to keep your pet at a healthy weight, and ask your veterinarian if your pet has a medical condition or a special diet.

## Daily amounts for adult dogs

Amounts are in grams per day, for the adult dog's ideal (not current) body weight. Split the daily amount into two meals.

| Ideal body weight | Grams per day |
|---|---|
| 5 kg | 95 g |
| 10 kg | 160 g |
| 15 kg | 215 g |
| 20 kg | 265 g |
| 30 kg | 360 g |
| 40 kg | 445 g |

Adjust for activity:

- Very active or working dogs: add **10–20%**.
- Neutered dogs and dogs over 8 years old: reduce by about **10%**.

## Daily amounts for adult cats

Amounts are in grams per day. Most cats do best with several small meals, so you can split the daily amount into two to four portions.

| Ideal body weight | Grams per day |
|---|---|
| 3 kg | 45 g |
| 4 kg | 55 g |
| 5 kg | 65 g |
| 6 kg | 75 g |

Indoor or neutered cats usually need about **10% less** than the table shows.

## Puppies and kittens

Puppies and kittens need a growth formula. Use **PawPantry Kitchen Puppy** or **PawPantry Kitchen Kitten** and follow the table printed on the bag, which is based on age and expected adult weight. Feed puppies three to four meals a day until 6 months of age, then two meals a day. Switch to adult food at around 12 months for most dogs (up to 18 months for large breeds) and at 12 months for cats.

## Mixing dry and wet food

If you also feed wet food, reduce the dry food: for dogs, remove about **25 g of dry food for every 100 g of wet food**; for cats, remove about **20 g of dry food for every 85 g pouch**.

## Treats

Treats should not exceed **10% of your pet's daily calories**. On days with many treats, reduce the meal slightly.

## Switching to a new food

Change food gradually over **7 days** to avoid an upset stomach:

| Days | Old food | New food |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–2 | 75% | 25% |
| Days 3–4 | 50% | 50% |
| Days 5–6 | 25% | 75% |
| Day 7 | 0% | 100% |

If your pet has a sensitive stomach, take 10–14 days instead. If your pet refuses the new food, our Happy Tummy Promise may apply (see *Returns & Refunds*).

## Water

Always provide fresh, clean water. Pets fed only dry food drink considerably more than pets fed wet food. Cats often drink more from moving water, which is why many owners use a water fountain.

## Storing dry food

- Keep the food in its **original bag**: the inner lining protects it from air and light. If you use a storage container, put the whole bag inside rather than pouring the kibble in.
- Close the bag after each use and store it in a cool, dry place below **25 °C**.
- Use an opened bag within **6 weeks**. Unopened bags keep until the best-before date printed on the back of the bag.
- Wash food bowls daily with hot water.

## Checking your pet's weight

Weigh your pet every 4 weeks. You should be able to feel the ribs easily without seeing them. If your pet gains or loses more than **5% of body weight**, adjust the daily amount by about 10% and check again after 4 weeks.
