# Returns & Refunds

We want you and your pet to be happy with every order. This page explains what you can return, how long you have, and when you receive your money back.

## Return window

You can return most items within **30 days of the delivery date**, as long as they are unused, unopened and in their original packaging. For orders delivered in several parcels, the 30 days start from the delivery of each parcel.

To start a return, go to **My account → Orders**, select the order and click **Return items**. You will receive a return label by email within one business day. Pack the items securely, attach the label and drop the parcel at any pick-up point of our carrier.

## Return shipping costs

- **France:** the return label costs €5.95. The amount is deducted from your refund.
- **Other EU countries:** the return label costs €8.95, deducted from your refund.
- **Switzerland and the United Kingdom:** we cannot provide prepaid labels. Please send the parcel back at your own cost with a tracked service, and include your order number.
- Returns are **free** when the item is faulty, damaged on arrival, or not what you ordered.

## Items that cannot be returned

For hygiene and safety reasons, the following items cannot be returned once opened:

- food, treats and supplements;
- cat litter;
- flea, tick and worming treatments;
- grooming products such as shampoos and ear cleaners.

Personalised items (for example engraved ID tags) and gift cards cannot be returned at all, unless they are faulty.

## The Happy Tummy Promise (opened food)

Pets can be picky. If your pet refuses to eat a food from our own **PawPantry Kitchen** range, you can ask for a refund even though the bag is open, as long as:

1. you contact us within **14 days of delivery**;
2. at least **half of the bag** is left; and
3. you have not used the Happy Tummy Promise for the same product before (it applies once per product per customer).

You do not need to send the food back. Simply send us a photo of the bag showing the batch number and remaining contents, and we refund the full price of that item. The Happy Tummy Promise applies only to the PawPantry Kitchen range, not to other brands we sell.

## Damaged or wrong items

If something arrives damaged, leaking or is not what you ordered, report it within **48 hours of delivery** with photos of the item and the packaging. Use **My account → Orders → Report a problem** or contact customer care. For food and litter we usually do not need the item back: we ship a free replacement or refund you, whichever you prefer. For accessories and devices, we send you a free return label.

Faults that appear later on devices such as fountains and feeders are handled under the warranty (see *Product Warranty*).

## Refund timing

We inspect returned items within **5 business days** of receiving them at our warehouse and then issue the refund. You receive a confirmation email when the refund is issued.

Refunds always go back to the original payment method:

- cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay: the money usually appears within 3–5 business days after we issue the refund, depending on your bank;
- PayPal: usually within 24 hours;
- bank transfer: within 5 business days, to the account the payment came from;
- items paid with a gift card are refunded as store credit, which never expires.

The original shipping cost is refunded only if you return the whole order because it was faulty, damaged or wrong.

## Exchanges

We do not offer direct exchanges. To swap an item for another size or flavour, return the original item and place a new order. If the new item is out of stock, our team can reserve it for 48 hours while your return is on its way — just ask.

## Subscriptions (Autoship)

Items delivered through Autoship follow the same return rules. Returning an item does not cancel your Autoship; you can change or cancel it at any time (see *Subscription Orders*).
